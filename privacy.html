Privacy Policy

FinTrak Pro · Effective 24 October 2026

The short version

  • Your finance records are private to your account. Database rules stop other users from reading them.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads and do not use tracking or analytics tools.
  • We do not store bank or card numbers, and we never ask for your GCash or Maya PIN.
  • Fingerprint and Face ID stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • You can download all your data, or delete your account and records, at any time from the Account page.

1. Who we are

FinTrak Pro is a personal finance tracker available at fintrakpro.github.io and as a mobile app. FinTrak Pro is responsible for your personal information under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Questions, requests or complaints: fintrakpro@gmail.com. This address also reaches our data protection officer.

3. How we use it

  • To provide FinTrak Pro: save your records, show your dashboard and create statements and reports.
  • To send your monthly finance summary by email. You can turn this off on the Account page.
  • To approve new accounts, confirm payments and answer support requests.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.

We process your information because you asked us to provide the service (our agreement with you), with your consent for optional details, and where the law requires it. We do not use your information for advertising or automated decisions about you.

4. Who can see your information

You

Only you can see your finance records in the app.

The FinTrak Pro administrator

To approve accounts and confirm payments, the administrator can see your account and profile details, access status, payment requests and history, and the number of entries you have. The administrator's tools do not show your transactions or other finance records.

Service providers who help us run FinTrak Pro

  • Supabase: secure database, sign-in and email-report hosting.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website. Like any web host, it receives your device's internet address when you visit.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs: deliver standard code libraries the website uses. The mobile app has these built in.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Our email delivery service: sends sign-up, password-reset and monthly report emails.

These providers handle data only to provide their service to us and must keep it secure. Some of them store data outside the Philippines; we use providers with strong security safeguards.

No one else

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it with authorities only when Philippine law requires it.

5. How we protect it

  • Encrypted connections: all data travels over HTTPS.
  • Private by design: database rules tie every record to its owner, so other users cannot read or change it.
  • Passwords: stored only in hashed form. No one, including us, can see your password.
  • App lock: optional fingerprint or Face ID lock in the mobile app. Your biometrics never leave your phone.
  • Payments: you pay through your own GCash or Maya app. We record only the reference number and amount, never PINs or card details.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever puts your information at risk, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of learning of it, as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile and finance records are permanently deleted. We may keep a minimal record of payments where Philippine law requires it, and nothing more.

Data saved on your phone (such as the App lock setting) is removed when you uninstall the app.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to erase or block, to data portability, and to claim damages. In the app you can:

  • Access and portability: Account → Download my data
  • Correct: Account → Profile & Personal Information
  • Erase: Account → Clear all my data or Delete my account
  • Object to e-mail reports: Account → Monthly e-mail report

For anything else, email fintrakpro@gmail.com. We reply within 15 days. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

8. Children

FinTrak Pro is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information, we will tell you in the app or by email first.
